Indicator Pack

The Indicator Pack includes all indicators used in Aurexion Labs strategies, plus a few extra to enhance your trading analysis and execution. Quantower already has a lot of built-in indicators, but these are custom made by us and does not yet exist in Quantower (as of 2025-09-24). There is a variation of Anchored VWAP, but it is more like an overlay that cannot be used in strategies, and you have to manually change the anchor point. Our version is automatically anchored to 3 different points during the day and will update without intervention, and it can be used in strategies.

Included Indicators:

  • AVWAP (Auto-anchored VWAP): Auto-anchored VWAP with customizable anchor points, flat logic, lookback, and band settings.
  • BBWP (Bollinger Band Width Percentage): Bollinger Band Width Percentage for trend and volatility analysis.
  • KER (Kaufman Efficiency Ratio): Identifies trending versus choppy markets.
  • GCF (Goerzel Chop Filter): Detects sideways/choppy markets to filter entries.
